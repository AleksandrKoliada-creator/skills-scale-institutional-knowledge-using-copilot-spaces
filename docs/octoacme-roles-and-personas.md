# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Lead / Product Owner

### Role Summary
Product Leads and Product Owners translate customer and business needs into a clear product direction, prioritized backlog, and measurable outcomes. They ensure the team is building the right thing at the right time.

### Responsibilities
- Define the product vision, priorities, and success metrics
- Own backlog refinement and priority decisions with the delivery team
- Clarify acceptance criteria and trade-offs for scope and sequencing
- Validate that shipped work addresses user and business needs
- Act as the primary link between stakeholders, Product Managers, and engineering

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Works with Developers and Engineering Lead to confirm scope feasibility and sequencing
- Coordinates with Project Managers on timelines, release scope, and dependencies
- Aligns with Sponsors and Stakeholders on strategic priorities and investment decisions
- Provides the product context that Product Managers use to drive roadmap and outcome measurement

### Typical Communication
- Backlog reviews and roadmap updates
- Stakeholder briefings and prioritization discussions
- Acceptance reviews and release readiness checks

---

## Engineering Lead / Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads guide technical direction, architecture, and implementation quality. They help the team make sound product decisions while balancing delivery speed, reliability, and maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction, architecture, and implementation standards
- Identify technical dependencies, risks, and constraints early
- Support estimation, planning, and sequencing with the delivery team
- Review design decisions and ensure code quality and maintainability
- Partner with Product and Project roles on trade-offs and delivery commitments

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Works closely with Developers to translate product intent into implementation plans
- Coordinates with Product Leads on the feasibility of scope and timeline trade-offs
- Supports Project Managers in dependency management and risk mitigation
- Helps QA/Test Lead define testability and release readiness requirements

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Dependency and risk updates in sprint planning and weekly syncs
- Implementation trade-off discussions with product and project stakeholders

---

## QA / Test Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Test Leads define quality standards, validate acceptance criteria, and support the team in delivering reliable outcomes. They focus on ensuring releases are ready for user and business use.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy, quality gates, and acceptance validation approach
- Track defects, triage issues, and coordinate resolution priorities
- Validate release readiness against critical user journeys and acceptance criteria
- Promote testability and quality practices across design and implementation work
- Partner with product and engineering leads on quality risks and mitigation

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Reviews work with Developers and Engineering Lead to confirm quality expectations and coverage
- Confirms that Product Lead expectations are met through user-focused validation
- Coordinates with Project Managers on test planning, release gates, and risk communication
- Escalates release-blocking issues to the appropriate Sponsor or stakeholder as needed

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and release readiness reviews
- Defect triage and issue prioritization meetings
- Quality sign-off checkpoints before deployment

---

## Delivery Manager / Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Delivery Managers or Scrum Masters keep the team aligned, remove blockers, and protect the delivery flow. They enable a predictable and productive execution rhythm across teams and stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, daily execution, and retrospective practices
- Remove blockers and improve team flow across workstreams
- Track delivery health, dependencies, and cross-team coordination
- Support adherence to team rituals, commitments, and Definition of Done
- Help surface risk early and keep work visible to stakeholders

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Coordinates with Project Managers on schedule, dependency, and communication cadence
- Partners with Product Lead to keep backlog prioritization and work sequencing aligned
- Supports Developers and Engineering Lead in resolving impediments and protecting focus
- Shares status clarity with Sponsors and Stakeholders without creating unnecessary escalation noise

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups, sprint planning, and retrospectives
- Dependency tracking and blocker reviews
- Team health and delivery status updates for project stakeholders

---

## Sponsor / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide strategic sponsorship, funding alignment, and executive-level support for the initiative. They help ensure the project remains aligned to business priorities and can navigate organizational barriers.

### Responsibilities
- Sponsor the initiative and align it with business strategy
- Support funding, staffing, and organizational prioritization
- Resolve cross-functional escalations and strategic trade-offs
- Review progress against business outcomes and major milestones
- Ensure the project has executive attention when needed for risk or change

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Works with Product Managers and Product Leads on strategic alignment and outcome validation
- Provides direction to Project Managers when high-level decisions or escalations are needed
- Supports Engineering and QA leaders when resource, policy, or business constraints affect delivery
- Receives concise status updates from PM and PMO-style coordination structures

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and executive updates
- Escalation calls for risk, funding, or policy issues
- Steering committee or sponsor briefings

---

## Stakeholder / Business Champion

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Business Champions represent the customer, business function, or operational need impacted by the work. They provide input, validate value, and help ensure outcomes meet real-world needs.

### Responsibilities
- Share domain knowledge, constraints, and operational context
- Validate that the solution addresses user and business needs
- Provide feedback on priorities, trade-offs, and acceptance criteria
- Participate in milestone reviews and decision-making where needed
- Support adoption and change readiness for the delivered work

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Partners with Product Lead and Product Manager on prioritization and value definition
- Reviews outcomes with Developers and QA to confirm business readiness and usability
- Coordinates with Project Managers to align timing and communication expectations
- Helps Sponsors understand stakeholder sentiment, operational impacts, and adoption needs

### Typical Communication
- Requirement reviews and feedback sessions
- milestone validation and release review discussions
- adoption and stakeholder readiness updates

---

## Cross-role interaction model

These personas work best when responsibilities are clear and handoffs are intentional:
- Product Lead and Product Manager ensure business value is clear and prioritized
- Project Manager and Delivery Manager keep execution structured, visible, and on schedule
- Engineering Lead and Developers turn priorities into reliable implementation
- QA/Test Lead validates outcomes against business and technical expectations
- Sponsor and Stakeholder roles provide decision support, alignment, and business confidence

This model improves clarity, accountability, and outcomes by making ownership explicit across planning, execution, validation, and release.
